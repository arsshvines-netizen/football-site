Brand kit

Logo

The icon shows a curved path (orange arrow) starting from a ball and moving forward over the centre circle of a pitch: progress, step by step. Original design.

Colour palette

  • Pitch green#0B6E4F · Primary
  • Energy orange#F26B21 · Secondary
  • Night navy#0D1B2A · Dark / text
  • Off-white#F7F9FA · Light
  • Highlight yellow#FFC857 · Accent
  • Deep orange#B8460C · Orange for text

Typography

Headings: extra-bold system sans-serif (same family as the body)

Train smarter. Play better.

Body text: system sans-serif, 16px, line height 1.6

Clear, friendly text that's easy to read on any phone, tablet or laptop.

Buttons and badges

Primary button Green button Outline button Beginner Intermediate Advanced

Style rules

  • Bold, UPPERCASE headings. Angled (diagonal) section edges, inspired by pitch markings.
  • Rounded cards with a coloured top edge. Plenty of white space.
  • Smooth, short animations (fade-up on scroll, rolling ball, counters). Turned off for people who choose reduced motion.
  • Orange buttons always use navy text (white on orange is too hard to read).
  • Never use orange text on green, or yellow text on white.
  • All text meets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast (at least 4.5:1). Full table: docs/branding-checklist.md.
  • Only original SVG illustrations and the founder's own photos. No club crests, no copyrighted images.
  • Friendly, encouraging, clear voice. Written for 12-15 year olds.

Business and marketing plan

Target customer

Who
Aspiring footballers aged 12-15
Level
Beginners to club players
Goal
Get better, get selected, enjoy the game
Problem
No clear plan for their age, position and level
Also reaches
Parents (they decide and pay) and youth coaches

Value proposition

"For young footballers aged 12-15 who want to improve but don't know where to start, PITCHPATH is a free, structured training plan built around your position and level, so you always know what to train next and can see yourself improve."

Brand positioning

Our space: structured like an academy, but free and flexible like online videos.

  • Versus online videos: a plan with progression, not random tricks.
  • Versus academies: free to start; train when and where you want.
  • Versus adult fitness apps: made for footballers aged 12-15, with safe training.

Business model

Freemium. Starter is free forever. Plus (₹199/month) and Team (₹2,999/season) are shown as "Launching soon" to test demand before launch. Prices live in assets/js/data/plans.js.

Marketing channels

  • Instagram and YouTube Shorts: 30-second drill clips
  • Clubs and schools: posters with QR codes
  • Coaches: free session plans for squads
  • Word of mouth: badges and progress shared with teammates

Customer engagement

  • Challenge of the month (Home page)
  • Badges and streaks (Tracker)
  • Level-up checklists (Programmes)
  • Market-research questions (Feedback)
