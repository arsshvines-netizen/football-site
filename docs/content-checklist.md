## Programmes: 3 levels × 3 focuses = 9 programmes
| Level | Technical Skills | Game Understanding | Physical Development | Level-up checklist |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Beginner | Master the Ball | Read the Game | Build Your Engine | ✅ 6 items |
| Intermediate | Skills Under Pressure | Think Like a Team | Faster and Stronger | ✅ 6 items |
| Advanced | Elite Touch | Game Intelligence | Match Ready | ✅ 6 items |

Each programme: 4 weeks × 3 sessions = 12 sessions (108 in total). Each session has an aim, total time (45-75 min, checked automatically), warm-up, 2-3 main activities and a cool-down. Every week says what changes. Load increases slowly; Advanced "Match Ready" week 4 is a lighter recovery week.

Every drill has: name, position, level, focus, duration, players, equipment, space, setup, steps, coaching points, easier, harder, and an SVG diagram.

**Decision to check:** heading drills were left out on purpose, because youth heading practice is restricted by many football associations. A note on the Drills page explains this.

## Content safety (spec Section 9)
| Rule | Status |
|---|---|
| No weight-loss / dieting / calorie advice | ✅ none on the site |
| No supplements / protein powder / energy drink recommendations | ✅ site says they are NOT needed |
| No maximal lifting / heavy weights | ✅ bodyweight only |
| No training through pain | ✅ "stop if it hurts" on Fitness, drills popup, footer |
| Gradual load increase + rest days | ✅ week-by-week progression, 1-2 rest days in every schedule |
| "Ask a coach/parent/doctor" notes | ✅ Fitness, Nutrition, Programmes, footer |
| No body-image comparisons | ✅ |
