Why free first?

Reach every player

Many young players can't afford private coaching. A free plan means anyone with a ball and some space can train with structure.

Build trust

Players and parents can see the quality of the training before paying anything. Good results lead to word-of-mouth recommendations.

Pay for the personal part

The free plan is the same for everyone. Paid plans add things that take real time to make, like personal plans and video feedback.
