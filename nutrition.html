Fuel your game

Simple food and water tips to fuel your game. No diets, no counting.

  • Real food first
  • Water is best
  • Never skip breakfast

Good to know: supplements, energy drinks and protein powders are not needed for young players. Normal, balanced meals give you everything you need.

The football plate

A simple guide for a training-day meal. No measuring: just roughly fill your plate like this, and eat regular meals and snacks.

    Calcium and iron

    Calcium (milk, yoghurt, cheese, fortified plant milks) for strong bones. Iron (beans, lentils, leafy greens, meat, eggs) for energy.

    Don't skip meals

    You're training and growing. Eat regular meals and snacks, especially breakfast.

    Treats are OK

    No food is "banned". Enjoy treats sometimes, as part of a varied diet.

    Hydration

    1. All day Sip water regularly, not just at training.
    2. Before Have a drink in the hour or two before training.
    3. During Take sips of water at every break.
    4. After Keep drinking to replace what you sweated out.

    Hot day or sweating a lot? Drink more. Water is the best choice, and milk is a great drink after training.

    The pee colour check

    Look at the colour of your urine (pee) when you go to the toilet:

    • Pale straw: well hydrated 👍
    • Yellow: drink some water
    • Dark yellow: drink water now

    Some foods and vitamins change the colour. If you're worried, talk to a parent or doctor.
