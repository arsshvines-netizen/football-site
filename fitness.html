Fit for football

Run all game, win the race to the ball and stay strong. Safe fitness for ages 12-15, using your own bodyweight only.

  • Bodyweight only
  • Warm up every time
  • 1-2 rest days a week

    Speed and agility

    Do speed work near the start of a session, straight after the warm-up. Keep sprints short and rest fully. If you're slowing down, stop.

    Strength and stability

    Pick your level. Only move up when every rep has perfect form. Rest 60 seconds between sets. Two strength sessions a week is plenty.

    Bodyweight strength training is a normal, safe part of training for young people when the technique is good. Ask a coach to check your form when you try a new exercise.

    Stamina

    Football is stop-start, so intervals (hard, easy, hard again) work better than long slow runs. Small-sided games like 3v3 or 4v4 build stamina with the ball.

    Rest, sleep and cool-down

    🗓️

    Rest days

    Your body gets stronger between sessions. Have 1-2 full rest days a week, and never train hard the day before a match.

    🌙

    Listen to your body

    Very tired, sore for days or not enjoying football? You may need more rest. Tell your coach or parent and take an easier week.

    After every session: cool-down

    Walk or jog easily for 2-3 minutes, then stretch gently. Never bounce, and never stretch into pain.

      Stay safe: the golden rules

      🔥

      Always warm up

      Before every training session and match.

      ✋

      Stop if it hurts

      Never train through pain. Being tired is normal; sharp pain is not.

      🌱

      Safe surfaces

      Check for holes, glass, wet or icy patches, and wear the right boots.

      💧

      Drink water

      Before, during and after training.

      🗣️

      Tell an adult

      Tell a coach or parent if you feel unwell, dizzy or injured.

      🩺

      See a doctor

      If pain lasts more than a few days, keeps coming back, or there is swelling.

      📈

      Build up slowly

      Don't suddenly double how much you train. Growing bodies need time.
