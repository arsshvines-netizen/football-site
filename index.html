The problem we solve

The problem

  • Young players want to improve, but don't know what to practise or in what order.
  • Online videos show tricks, not a plan for your age, position and level.
  • Private coaching is expensive and only a few hours a week.

Our solution

  • A structured path from Beginner to Advanced, week by week.
  • Training for your position: keeper, defender, midfielder, winger or striker.
  • Free to start, safe for ages 12-15, with a tracker to see your progress.

How it works

  1. 1

    Choose your level

    Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced. Each level tells you who it's for and what you need before you start.

  2. 2

    Train with the plan

    Follow a 4-week programme with 3 sessions a week, using drills made for your position.

  3. 3

    Track your progress

    Log every session, rate your skills and watch your charts grow. Then level up!
