My journey

  1. ⚽

    It started with a ball

    Like most players, I started by kicking a ball around with friends whenever I could. I never wanted to stop, and I wanted to get better every single time.

  2. ©

    Captain of my school team

    Being made captain taught me that football is about more than my own game. It's about lifting the players around me, staying calm under pressure and leading by example.

  3. 🛡

    Mumbai City FC

    Club football raised the level: proper training plans, real tactical work, fitness and recovery done right. It showed me how much faster you improve when you train with structure.

  4. ★

    The national stage

    Representing Mumbai and ISSO at the SGFI National Games meant playing against some of the best young footballers in the country. Every match was a lesson.

  5. →

    Starting PITCHPATH

    Along the way I saw so many talented young players who wanted to improve but had no plan. So I built one, and made it free to start.

Why I built this

"Talent gets you noticed. A plan gets you better. I want every young player to have that plan, not just the ones at big academies."

Everything on PITCHPATH comes from what has worked for me: training that builds week by week, drills made for your position, smart fitness, good food and rest, and keeping track of your progress so you can see how far you've come.

What football taught me

Show up every day

Improvement doesn't come from one great session. It comes from many good ones, week after week.

Lead by example

As captain, the way I train sets the tone. Work hard, stay positive, help your teammates.

Train smart, rest well

The best players plan their training, look after their body and never train through pain.
