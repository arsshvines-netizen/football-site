Why I started PITCHPATH

Football has been a huge part of my life for years. Training at club level, I've been through a lot of different programmes, drills and fitness routines, and I've seen how much a clear plan helps a player improve.

But I noticed that many young players starting out don't have that. They want to get better, but they don't know what to practise, in what order, or what's right for their position.

So I built the plan I'd give them: structured programmes for every level, drills for every position, safe fitness and food advice, and a tracker to see the progress. And it's free to start.
