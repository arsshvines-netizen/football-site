About the brand

Every player needs a path

A football training business founded by a player, for players.

PITCHPATH: Train with a plan.

Our story

PITCHPATH was founded by Arssh Gupta, a young club footballer who noticed a problem: beginners who wanted to get better at football often didn't know what to practise, or in what order.

Online videos show amazing skills, but they don't give you a plan. Club training is great, but it's only a few hours a week. So this site was built to give young players a clear, safe, step-by-step path they can follow on their own or with a coach.

Mission and values

Mission: to give every young footballer a free, structured and safe plan to improve, and a way to see their progress.

Structure

Clear plans that build step by step.

Safety

Age-appropriate training, real food and rest.

Effort

Progress comes from showing up and trying.

Inclusion

For every player, every position, every level.

Who it's for and what makes it different

Who it's for

  • Young aspiring footballers aged 12-15
  • Players training on their own, or with a parent, friend or coach
  • Beginners through to club players who want more
  • Coaches looking for simple, ready-made sessions

What makes it different

  • Structured progression: 3 levels, 9 programmes, a level-up checklist
  • Position-specific: 40 drills across 5 positions
  • Free: no sign-up, no adverts, no payments
  • Tracks progress: a private tracker with charts

Style rules

  • Bold, UPPERCASE headings. Angled (diagonal) section edges, inspired by pitch markings.
  • Rounded cards with a coloured top edge. Plenty of white space.
  • Orange buttons always use navy text (white on orange is too hard to read).
  • Never use orange text on green, or yellow text on white.
  • All text meets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast (at least 4.5:1).
  • Only CSS/SVG illustrations: no club crests, no real player photos, no copyrighted images.
  • Friendly, encouraging, clear voice. Written for 12-15 year olds.

Meet the founder

Arssh Gupta

Founder · Club footballer · Captain, number 9

Football has been one of my main interests for years. I play regularly, both with my club and for fun with friends. Through that I've used and gone through many different training programmes, drills and fitness routines.

I noticed that young footballers starting their football journey often find it hard to get a structured training plan for their age, position and level. I'm also really interested in business: how companies understand their customers, build a brand and turn an idea into a real product.

PITCHPATH brings those two interests together. It's a football training brand built around what young players actually need.

Value proposition

"For young footballers aged 12-15 who want to improve but don't know where to start, PITCHPATH is a free, structured training plan built around your position and level, so you always know what to train next and can see yourself improve."

Brand positioning

Our space: structured like an academy, but free and flexible like online videos.

  • Versus online videos: we give a plan with progression, not random tricks.
  • Versus academies: free to start, and you train when and where you want.
  • Versus adult fitness apps: made for footballers aged 12-15, with safe, age-appropriate training.

Marketing channels

  • Instagram and YouTube Shorts: 30-second drill videos and challenge clips, where young players already spend time.
  • Clubs and school teams: posters in changing rooms and QR codes on training kit bags.
  • Coaches: free session plans they can share with their squads.
  • Word of mouth: players sharing their badges and progress with teammates.

Channels chosen using our survey of young footballers aged 12-15.

Customer engagement

  • Challenge of the month: a new goal every month to keep players coming back.
  • Badges and streaks: rewards in the tracker for training consistently.
  • Level-up checklists: a clear next goal at every level.
  • Feedback loop: testers' ideas shape new drills and the paid plans.
