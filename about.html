About the brand

Every player needs a path

PITCHPATH: Train with a plan.

Our story

PITCHPATH was created by a young player who noticed a problem: beginners who wanted to get better at football often didn't know what to practise, or in what order.

Online videos show amazing skills, but they don't give you a plan. Club training is great, but it's only a few hours a week. So this site was built to give young players a clear, safe, step-by-step path they can follow on their own or with a coach.

Mission and values

Mission: to give every young footballer a free, structured and safe plan to improve, and a way to see their progress.

Structure

Clear plans that build step by step.

Safety

Age-appropriate training, real food and rest.

Effort

Progress comes from showing up and trying.

Inclusion

For every player, every position, every level.

Who it's for and what makes it different

Who it's for

  • Young aspiring footballers aged 12-15
  • Players training on their own, or with a parent, friend or coach
  • Beginners through to club players who want more
  • Coaches looking for simple, ready-made sessions

What makes it different

  • Structured progression: 3 levels, 9 programmes, a level-up checklist
  • Position-specific: 40 drills across 5 positions
  • Free: no sign-up, no adverts, no payments
  • Tracks progress: a private tracker with charts

Style rules

  • Bold, UPPERCASE headings. Angled (diagonal) section edges, inspired by pitch markings.
  • Rounded cards with a coloured top edge. Plenty of white space.
  • Orange buttons always use navy text (white on orange is too hard to read).
  • Never use orange text on green, or yellow text on white.
  • All text meets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast (at least 4.5:1).
  • Only CSS/SVG illustrations: no club crests, no real player photos, no copyrighted images.
  • Friendly, encouraging, clear voice. Written for 12-15 year olds.
